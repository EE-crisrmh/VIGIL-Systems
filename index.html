An autonomous drone that patrols the dark, so nobody has to.

VIGIL patrols a warehouse at night alongside the security guard, finds people with a thermal camera and on-board AI, and alerts the guard. The guard stays in charge.

The problem

Overnight security is risky and expensive. A guard patrolling a large, dark building alone can run into an intruder with no backup, and at one site we interviewed nobody patrols for 2 to 3 hours between rounds. Fixed cameras and alarms can report that something happened, but they can't go and look.

VIGIL fills that gap: a drone that checks the building, finds people in the dark, and alerts the guard. It is an extra layer, not a replacement. Our first customers are large warehouses.

How it works

Patrols on its own

The building is mapped into zones and the drone picks its next waypoint at random, weighted toward zones not visited recently. No fixed route, no predictable gaps.

Sees in the dark

A thermal camera and LiDAR feed an AI person detector that runs on board.

Flies without GPS

LiDAR mapping and optical flow hold its position indoors.

Alerts the guard

When it spots a likely person, it alerts the guard. We are still deciding the alert method.

Indoor only. No face matching. Photos are taken on a trigger, not continuously. No remote piloting.

Where we are

VIGIL is a prototype in development. We are building on a Pixhawk 6C Mini flight controller running PX4, with a Jetson Orin Nano Super companion computer running ROS 2 for detection and patrol logic. Our test airframe is assembled and we are working toward a full patrol demo.

We are applying to accelerator programs this cycle and talking to investors, mentors and warehouse operators.
